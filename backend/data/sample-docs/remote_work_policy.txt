Guidely Inc. Remote Work Policy
1. Working Hours & Core Availability: All full-time employees are expected to be reachable via Slack and email during core business hours (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time).
2. Equipment Stipend: Remote employees receive a one-time  home office setup allowance upon hire and a  monthly internet reimbursement.
3. Travel & Offsites: Company-wide offsites occur twice a year. Travel and lodging costs are fully covered by Guidely.
