Guidely Inc. PTO and Leave Policy
1. Vacation Days: Employees receive 20 paid leave days annually, accrued on a monthly basis. Up to 5 unused PTO days can roll over into the next calendar year.
2. Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. No doctor note is required for absences under 3 consecutive business days.
3. Parental Leave: Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks.
